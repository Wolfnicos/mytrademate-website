🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 29, 2025

Your Privacy Matters. MyTradeMate Portfolio is built on a foundation of privacy and security. We believe your portfolio data is yours alone. This policy explains how we protect your information.

🎯 Our Privacy Philosophy

MyTradeMate Portfolio operates on a simple principle: Local AI processing, maximum privacy. We designed our app to minimize data collection and maximize your security. All AI models run 100% on your device using TensorFlow Lite. Your portfolio data, API keys, and all processing happen locally - we don't have backend servers to collect or store your information.

🤖 Local AI Processing (Zero Tracking)

MyTradeMate Portfolio works differently from other crypto apps:

  • ✅ AI runs on your device: TensorFlow Lite models process 76 technical indicators locally
  • ✅ Works offline: 1000 candles cached locally, predictions work without internet
  • ✅ Direct API calls: Your device connects directly to Binance API (no intermediary servers)
  • ✅ No MyTradeMate servers: We don't operate backend servers for user data
  • ✅ Zero tracking: No analytics, telemetry, or data collection

📊 What We DON'T Collect

Unlike most crypto apps, MyTradeMate Portfolio does NOT collect or store:

  • API Keys - Your Binance read-only API credentials are encrypted and stored ONLY on your device
  • Portfolio Data - Your balances, holdings, and performance data never leave your device
  • Personal Information - No email, phone number, or identity verification required
  • Financial Data - We never see your balances, profits, or losses
  • Location Data - No GPS tracking or location monitoring
  • Behavioral Data - No tracking pixels, analytics, or user profiling

✅ What We DO Collect (Almost Nothing)

To provide basic app functionality:

  • Subscription Status ONLY - Managed by Google Play (not us) to verify your subscription

That's it. We do NOT collect crash reports, analytics, device info, or any telemetry. If something breaks, we rely on you emailing us directly at mytrademate.app@gmail.com rather than automated tracking.

🔐 How We Protect Your Data

Local Encryption

Your Binance read-only API keys are encrypted using AES-256 encryption and stored in your device's secure keychain (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). We never transmit your keys to our servers.

Biometric Security

You can enable Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint authentication to add an extra layer of protection. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is managed by Apple/Google security systems.

No Cloud Sync, No Servers

Your portfolio data, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We don't sync data to the cloud or operate backend servers. Your device connects directly to Binance API for price data - nothing goes through our servers because we don't have any.

⚠️ Important: Read-Only API Key

When creating your Binance API key for MyTradeMate Portfolio, you MUST use these settings:

  • Enable: Read permissions ONLY (to view balances and portfolio)
  • Disable: ALL trading permissions (Spot, Margin, Futures)
  • Disable: Withdrawals (always disabled for security)
  • Optional: IP Access Restrictions (whitelist only your IP for extra security)

This app is designed for portfolio tracking only. We cannot and will not execute any transactions, trades, or withdrawals. Your read-only API key ensures maximum security.

🍪 Cookies and Tracking (ZERO)

MyTradeMate Portfolio does NOT use:

  • Advertising cookies
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
  • Cross-site tracking or fingerprinting
  • ANY analytics or telemetry in the app

Zero tracking. Period. We built this app to respect your privacy completely. If you need help, email us directly - we don't use automated tracking to spy on your usage.

🤝 Third-Party Services (Direct Connections)

Important: Your device connects DIRECTLY to these services. No data flows through MyTradeMate servers (we don't have any).

Binance API (Direct Connection from Your Device)

When you use your read-only API keys, YOUR DEVICE communicates directly with Binance servers to fetch portfolio balances and market data. This communication is encrypted (HTTPS) and follows Binance's own privacy policy. MyTradeMate does NOT act as an intermediary. We can ONLY view your portfolio - we cannot access your funds or execute any transactions.

CoinGecko API (Optional Coin Logos)

Your device may fetch cryptocurrency logos directly from CoinGecko's public API. No personal information is shared with CoinGecko. This is optional for visual display only.

Google Play Services (Subscription Only)

Payment processing and subscription management are handled entirely by Google Play Store. Only Google sees your payment information - we never do. Google's privacy policy governs their data practices.
