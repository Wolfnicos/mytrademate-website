🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 26, 2025

Your Privacy Matters. MyTradeMate is built on a foundation of privacy and security. We believe your trading data is yours alone. This policy explains how we protect your information.

🎯 Our Privacy Philosophy

MyTradeMate operates on a simple principle: Your keys, your crypto, your data. We designed our app to minimize data collection and maximize your privacy. All sensitive information stays on your device, encrypted and secure.

📊 What We DON'T Collect

Unlike most trading apps, MyTradeMate does NOT collect or store:

  • API Keys - Your Binance API credentials are encrypted and stored ONLY on your device
  • Trading History - Your trades, orders, and portfolio data never leave your device
  • Personal Information - No email, phone number, or identity verification required
  • Financial Data - We never see your balances, profits, or losses
  • Location Data - No GPS tracking or location monitoring
  • Behavioral Data - No tracking pixels, analytics, or user profiling

✅ What We DO Collect (Minimal)

To provide basic app functionality, we collect only:

  • Crash Reports - Anonymous error logs to fix bugs (optional, can be disabled)
  • App Version - To ensure compatibility and updates
  • Device Type - iOS or Android, for optimization purposes only

All of this data is anonymous and cannot be linked back to you personally.

🔐 How We Protect Your Data

Local Encryption

Your Binance API keys are encrypted using AES-256 encryption and stored in your device's secure keychain (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). We never transmit your keys to our servers.

Biometric Security

You can enable Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint authentication to add an extra layer of protection. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is managed by Apple/Google security systems.

No Cloud Sync

Your trading data, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We don't sync data to the cloud, meaning you have complete control over your information.

⚠️ Important: API Key Permissions

When creating your Binance API key for MyTradeMate, we recommend these settings:

  • Enable: Read permissions (to view balances and orders)
  • Enable: Spot & Margin Trading (only if you want to trade)
  • Disable: Withdrawals (for maximum security)
  • Enable: IP Access Restrictions (whitelist only your IP)

This ensures that even if your device is compromised, attackers cannot withdraw funds from your account.

🍪 Cookies and Tracking

MyTradeMate does NOT use:

  • Advertising cookies
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
  • Cross-site tracking or fingerprinting

Our website uses minimal technical cookies only for basic functionality (like remembering your language preference).

🤝 Third-Party Services

MyTradeMate connects to these third-party services:

Binance API

When you use your API keys, the app communicates directly with Binance servers to fetch market data and execute trades. This communication is encrypted (HTTPS) and follows Binance's own privacy policy.

RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

For in-app purchases and subscriptions, we use RevenueCat. They collect minimal data (transaction ID, subscription status) to manage your premium access. RevenueCat is GDPR and CCPA compliant.

👤 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliant)

Under data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of any data we hold (which is essentially zero)
  • Delete: Request deletion of your data (can be done by uninstalling the app)
  • Opt-Out: Disable crash reporting and analytics in app settings
  • Portability: Export your data at any time (saved locally on your device)

👶 Children's Privacy

MyTradeMate is not intended for users under 18 years old. Cryptocurrency trading involves financial risk and is restricted to adults. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or website. Your continued use of MyTradeMate after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

🛡️ Bottom Line

MyTradeMate respects your privacy. We built this app for traders who value security and control. Your API keys, trading data, and personal information stay on your device, encrypted and protected. We can't access it, we don't want to access it, and we will never sell it.
